Source. Daily euro foreign-exchange reference rates published by the European Central Bank, served through the open-source Frankfurter API. Data is fetched in your browser every time this page loads, so results update automatically.

Construction. Daily rates are averaged within each calendar month; the current, incomplete month is dropped. Changes are 100 × log differences of consecutive monthly averages. A positive change means the dollar strengthened against that currency.

Method. OLS slope and intercept; heteroskedasticity- and autocorrelation-consistent (Newey-West, Bartlett kernel, 3 lags) standard errors with a small-sample n/(n−2) adjustment; two-sided p-values from Student's t with n−2 degrees of freedom.
